Frequently Asked Questions

What is VaahanPay?

VaahanPay is an online platform designed to help vehicle owners check available traffic challan information, review pending fines and manage eligible challan payments through a simpler digital experience.

How can I check my vehicle challan online?

Enter your vehicle registration number on VaahanPay and click Check Challan. Available challan information associated with your vehicle will then be displayed for review.

Can I check a challan using my vehicle number?

Yes. Simply enter your vehicle registration number, such as DL 01 AB 1234, to search for available traffic challan records associated with the vehicle.

Can I pay my e-challan online through VaahanPay?

Eligible challans may be available for online payment through VaahanPay. Available payment options will be shown after your challan details are retrieved.

Where does VaahanPay get challan information from?

VaahanPay uses available challan information from relevant traffic and government challan systems, including applicable Parivahan/eChallan records.

Can VaahanPay reduce my challan amount?

Certain challans may have eligible payment, settlement or savings options depending on applicable rules and circumstances. Any available option will be clearly displayed before you proceed.

A reduction in challan amount is not guaranteed.

Is VaahanPay a government website?

No. VaahanPay is an independent platform and is not a government website or government authority.

Is online challan payment safe?

VaahanPay uses secure technology and payment processes to help protect transaction information when making eligible challan payments.

What information do I need to check my challan?

In most cases, you can begin by entering your vehicle registration number. Additional information may be required depending on the challan or payment process.

Can I check challans for both cars and bikes?

Yes. Available challan information can be searched for supported cars, motorcycles, scooters and other registered vehicles.

What happens after I pay my challan?

After a successful eligible payment, you will receive the available transaction confirmation and can track the payment status through the applicable process.
